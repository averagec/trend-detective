About the case files

Mission: answer one honest question with NASA data. Is it really changing, where, by how much, and can we prove it?

You play a detective at an Earth system desk. Sea level next to 31 Indonesian cities comes from NASA's ECCO ocean model; temperature and rain come from NASA MERRA-2. Lock in a guess, look at the record, then test it against 1,000 trend-free fakes.

Data: ECCO V4r4 (NASA PO.DAAC) · MERRA-2 (NASA GES DISC) · NASA GIBS · Natural Earth. Full references in ABOUT.TXT on the desk.
