How a case works

  1. Pick the variable, the region and the years.
  2. Lock in your guess before you see anything.
  3. Plot the time series and judge it yourself.
  4. Test it against 1,000 trend-free fakes.
  5. Get a verdict: Detected, Not Yet Detectable, or Suspect.
